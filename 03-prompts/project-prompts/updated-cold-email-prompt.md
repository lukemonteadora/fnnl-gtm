## Instructions and Inspiration:

Consider the following when creating the email sequence:
1. How does each email directly address the ICP's specific pain points or goals?
2. How does each email align with the identified targeting angles?
3. What unique value proposition or quick win can we offer in each email?
4. How can we make each email irresistible to the specific decision-makers we're targeting?
5. How does the sequence naturally lead to the client's core product or service?

For Broad Campaigns:
- Focus on pain points and goals that are common across the wider ICP or industry
- Use more general language that can resonate with a diverse set of prospects
- Highlight versatile case studies or social proof that demonstrate wide applicability

For Highly Targeted Campaigns:
- Leverage specific data points (e.g., job postings, recent events) for extreme personalization
- Craft ultra-relevant offers or lead magnets that directly address the specific angle
- Use hyper-focused language that speaks directly to the prospect's immediate situation

## Rules for Cold Email:
1. Keep emails under 70 words (Ideally under 45 words). Not including email signatures.
2. Avoid spam words.
3. Clearly communicate the reason for reaching out.
4. Write at a 5th-grade level.
5. Make the message about them, not you.
6. Mention industry relevant case study or pain point.
7. Use social proof with quantitative results.
8. Use subject lines that could be from a colleague or customer (without lying).
9. Keep subject lines under 2-3 words, preferably lowercase.
10. Lead with a "why you, why now" sentence for preview text.
11. Personalize each email, even follow-ups.
12. Zero fluff words, every word has meaning
13. No meaningless "personalization"
14. Change the value proposition in follow-up emails.
15. Show, don't tell - demonstrate capabilities when possible.
16. Handle objections preemptively in the message.
17. Include a strong offer or valuable lead magnet.
