# Archive: offer-ideation limits removed (2026-09-22)

**Operator directive (2026-09-22):** stop limiting which offers get generated and tested because the client might not want them, because we or the client might not be able to deliver them at full volume, because they need a free trial or product access, or because they take effort or custom work per prospect. Follow Aaron Shepherd's order instead: research the buyer, generate a lot of offers, test three or four, let the market pick, and only then ask the client what it takes to run the winner at full volume. Other gates found by a five-part audit of the flow that blocked testing in the same way were fixed in the same pass.

## The one rule kept

Every prospect who says yes during a test gets what the email promised, on time. The agency honors test yeses by default. An offer only the client can honor (their product or a trial of it, a price or guarantee term, their people's time, their goods, licensed work) is generated and shown like any other, flagged "Needs your OK", and sends only after the client says yes. A client veto is always honored. Truth, compliance, deliverability and spend rails are unchanged.

## What changed, by stage

**New skill `/offer-test-slate`.** Two client decks: the offer test plan before the test (buyer types, problems in the buyer's words, every offer and why, who honors a yes, the test plan; approval optional by default via `run_state.offer_slate_approval`), and the results-and-scale review after it (what was tested, what won, what running the winner at full volume takes; the answer sizes the coverage wave).

**Offer creation.**
- `/lead-magnet-brainstorm`: the HARD "Who Delivers the Offer (Fulfillment Split)" gate became **Test First, Ask About Scale After**; every candidate stays visible and is ranked on buyer pull; "Scalable delivery" became "Perceived value" in the 7-point rubric; twenty-yes economics became a scale note; the Premium custom-asset tier deferral is retired; K1 (no tool in the first wave) and K2 (a funded, owned build before lock) were reworked; the weak-offer screen and why-not and farming checks label and rank instead of deleting; validation arms are uncapped; new per-offer fields `lead_magnet_yes_honored_by`, `lead_magnet_client_dependency`, `lead_magnet_client_ok`, `lead_magnet_scale_needs`, plus arm `flight`, `read`, and statuses `vetoed`, `hold`, `works`. The Signal-Capability Check no longer drops an offer because a locked-stack tool's key is not provisioned yet (A-Sales, ported from FNNL).
- `/offer-intelligence`: no delivery-capacity condition on directions; validation mode scores perceived value and checks fulfillment separately.
- FNNL only: retired web slots count as Claude-native capability in the Signal-Capability Check; ideation wording follows the no-AI-API rule; a missing disqualifier field never reopens a locked offer.

**Copy.** `/campaign-copywriting` Confirmed Deliverable Gate asks "can every yes be honored?" (agency by default, client with `lead_magnet_client_ok: yes`); only concrete #1 (the deliverable) is required before copy; a missing case study holds only the proof-led angle, never the slice; a pay-on-results term answers the micro-value question; "never manufactured" now means never promise an asset that will not exist, not "never build one". Matching updates in `outreach-copy-framework.md`, `cold-email-copy-best-practices.md`, `/lifecycle-copy`, `/outreach-copywriting`.

**Lists and research.** Proof facts no longer hold a group's list build (`/tam-build`, `/social-proof-bank`); `/lead-source-strategy` allows a broad pull for offer-test cells and treats capacity as a post-test question; `/lead-list-tech-stack-research` samples are data-quality checks, not reply tests; `verified-list-gate.md` accepts a validation-arm cell; `list-building-play-library.md` runs several offers per wave in randomized cells.

**Testing and launch.** `/campaign-strategy`: arms run in flights sized to send capacity, uncapped; only an arm whose yes can be honored today is scheduled; the winner is picked only on a fair, matched comparison and HOLD arms keep sending; arm cells are funded before positioning campaigns when capacity is short; the flip odds are stated honestly; scale review runs before the coverage wave. `/experiment-design` exempts offer-only tests from the 1% baseline rule and proposes the offer queue first. `/campaign-approval-brief` no longer demands approval of each new offer and mirrors the tested set. `/emailbison-campaign-upload-public` (A-Sales) reads `lead_magnet_client_ok` instead of hard-stopping.

**Orchestration and onboarding.** `/cold-email-kickoff`: new Step 3.2 (offer test slate) and Step 8 (scale review), resume rows, discovery on every segment (no top-3 cap), an existing offer can be the control beside new arms, a Start-time per-arm client-OK check in Gate C. `/cold-email-weekly-rhythm`: scale review trigger and a "Start next offer flight" move. Intake no longer requires the client to declare operational limits; new `offer_slate_approval` field. The "filter failed paths out" line now protects only untrue, unsafe or non-compliant paths in `/cold-email-kickoff`, `/icp-onboarding`, `/cold-email-starter-kit`, `/cold-email-weekly-rhythm`, `/lead-magnet-brainstorm`, `/offer-intelligence`, `/campaign-copywriting`. `/autonomous-execution-policy` locks the offer test set instead of one pick; `/continue-client` routes to the scale review; `/clarification-first`, `/cold-email-flow-audit`, `/client-onboarding-call-summarizer`, `first-wave-eric-defaults.md` and (A-Sales) `_OPERATOR-GUIDE.md` were aligned.
